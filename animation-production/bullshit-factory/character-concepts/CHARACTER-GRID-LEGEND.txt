STYLE RULES FOR BOTH SETS

- 16-bit-inspired pixel art
- Designed as modular, separable character parts later
- Strong silhouettes and readable poses
- Distinct movement signature for every character
- Distinct fictional hybrid voice for every human character
- The dog never speaks human words
- The dog communicates through expressive barks and physical acting
- Keep the brand self-contained and the cast identity specific to Bullshit Factory
